How this works

  1. Enter the product, your trading name, and each ingredient with a weight.
  2. Label14 sorts the list heaviest first and emphasises names it recognises from the UK’s 14 major allergens.
  3. It will not guess a blend. “Spice mix”, “seasoning” and similar lines are flagged for you to open the spec.
  4. Tick each detected allergen only after you have checked the supplier specification.
  5. Print a cake-box (A6) label or an Avery L7160-style 63.5 × 38.1 mm sheet from the browser.

Recipes are stored in this browser only (localStorage), up to 3 in the free v1. A paid unlock for more saved recipes is planned. There is no checkout in this version.

The 14 major allergens

From Food Standards Agency guidance. Only these must be emphasised as allergens under the rules. Other ingredients can still cause reactions — they are simply not on this statutory list.
